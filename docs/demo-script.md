# Two-minute demo talk track

**Video Gaming Agents — Turn footage into a playable story.**

Speak conversationally at roughly 160 words per minute. The deck lasts exactly 120 seconds. This is a draft for the next brainstorm.

## 00:00–00:12 / PRESS START

We shoot a lot of video. Making something worth watching is the hard part. What if editing felt like playing a game? Meet Video Gaming Agents: two agents, one adventure.

## 00:12–00:26 / THE FOOTAGE DUNGEON

Here is a real example: a visit to an AI data center. Server rooms, corridors, conversations, and one unexpected arcade. The good story is in there. First, we need to find the clues.

## 00:26–00:46 / CONTEXT AGENT

Our Context Agent sees what is on screen, hears what people say, and reads the file facts. Those clues come together in a searchable metadata index, linked back to the original footage. Now we can find a moment by what it means, instead of remembering its file name.

## 00:46–01:02 / STORY UNLOCKED

Add your context: what happened, what matters, and who the video is for. The agent combines that intent with the footage to suggest a hook and story beats. Here is our twist: an arcade hiding inside an AI data center. Save that story as a cartridge.

## 01:02–01:14 / LOAD YOUR QUEST

Pick a Story cartridge and plug it in. The Video Agent receives the story and its source references. You bring the creative direction. The cartridge brings the context, so you do not have to explain everything again.

## 01:14–01:34 / VIDEO AGENT

Ask for a thirty-second short, and lead with the arcade surprise. The Video Agent finds the relevant moments, plans the sequence, and assembles the edit. You can ask for tighter pacing, captions, or a different opening. Chat becomes your controller, and the preview becomes your feedback.

## 01:34–01:50 / GAME ON

Game on! The planned result moves from the server room to the arcade reveal, then lands on a playful payoff. This preview illustrates the concept; the editing backend is our next step. One Story cartridge could power several different cuts.

## 01:50–02:00 / QUEST COMPLETE

The Context Agent finds the meaning. The Video Agent makes the movie. Same footage, more stories. Less time lost searching, more time spent creating. Pick a cartridge. Press start.

## Stage direction

Open the deck on PRESS START. Play the continuous demo, or enable Pause after each level for live narration. Point at the three extraction branches as they converge, then the two inputs to Story composition. Let the cartridge insertion finish before introducing the Video Agent. GAME ON is an illustrated output, so describe it as the planned result. Finish on the two-agent handoff.
